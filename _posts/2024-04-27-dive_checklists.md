## Dressing order: sidemount

1. Regs fitted with regs on RHS and turret down
1. Tank valve opened
1. Breathe Air
1. Tank valve closed
1. Wetsuit zipped up
1. Put BCD on, left arm first
1. Crotch strap done up
1. Wing bungee through crotch strap
1. Pouch closed and on
1. SMB assembled and clipped on
1. Compass and watch on
1. Check watch is connected to transmitters
1. Tank valve opened
1. Straps untangled
1. Inflator attached
1. Hood up
1. Regulator necklace on
1. Long hose regulator on, and tucked in

## Dressing order: backmount

1. Assemble BCD
1. Inflator attached
1. Tank valve opened
1. Breathe Air
1. Wetsuit zipped up
1. Put BCD on, left arm first
1. Crotch strap done up
1. Pouch closed and on
1. SMB assembled and clipped on
1. Compass and watch on
1. Check watch is connected to transmitter
1. Straps untangled
1. Hood up
1. Regulator necklace on
1. Long hose regulator on, and tucked in
